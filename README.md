# Coffee-Shop-Sales-Customer-Behavior-Analysis-Dashboard
This project provides end-to-end insights into coffee shop transactions, evaluating key performance indicators (KPIs) to help optimize store inventory, pricing strategies, peak staffing hours, and sales distributions across different regions.



---

## Project Overview

This project provides end-to-end insights into coffee shop transactions, helping stakeholders track core business metrics, optimize store staffing during peak hours, adjust pricing and inventory, and analyze sales across product categories.

## Key Dashboard Insights

### 1. Product Performance
* **Top Categories:** Coffee is the highest revenue category, followed by Tea, Bakery, Drinking Chocolate, and Coffee Beans.
* **Underperforming Categories:** Branded products, Loose Tea, Flavours, and Packaged Chocolate generate lower total sales.
* **Top Individual Products:** Led by Barista Espresso, Brewed Chai Tea, Hot Chocolate, and Gourmet Brewed Coffee.

### 2. Time & Day Analysis
* **Peak Hours:** Morning (6 AM – 12 PM) accounts for 55.72% ($66.27K) of total revenue.
* **Secondary Hours:** Afternoon drives 29.09% ($34.6K) and Evening accounts for 15.19% ($18.07K).
* **Daily Sales Trend:** Revenue peaks on weekends (Sunday and Saturday), with lower, steady performance through weekdays.

### 3. Location Revenue Breakdown
* **Astoria:** $40.3K (33.89%)
* **Lower Manhattan:** $39.48K (33.19%)
* **Hell's Kitchen:** $39.16K (32.92%)
*(Revenue is evenly distributed across all three locations)*
